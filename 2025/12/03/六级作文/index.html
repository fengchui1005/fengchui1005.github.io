方法论概述

基本六级的作文都是可以归结为下面的思路

  • 给出一个现象
    • 通过产生现象的原因的，如何解决来得到写作文的话题
    • 比如25年的 As social media is used more and more extensively,there is a growing awareness of the importance of using it properly and responsibly. 很容易得出作文的topic 是什么 ，就是the importance of using it properly and responsibly 分为几个方向去写，具体的选择就是你用那个模板
      • 问题解决
        • 如何去做
      • 现象解释
        • 好处
        • 为什么
        • 。。。
    • 第三段
      • 这里可以套模板，具体化对应的词，一般就是 个人和世界社会的作用这些美好的东西

论证的话，具体还是要积累句型，知道，更要练习

把作文中的大而空的内容

  • 具体化
  • 实例化
  • 描写出来

模板

开头

Undoubtedly, it is imperative for individuals to place emphasis on / pay attention to XXXX.

It is a sober reminder that individuals ought to place emphasis on / pay attention to XXXX.

In my opinion , XXXX play a pivotal role in XXX (champion the idea that XXXX possess considerable significance )

中间论述

  • 长短结合
  • 短句直接表达观点
  • 长句表达观点
  • 写2-3句

The value of XXXX can not be overstated as evidenced by the following reasons.(There are multiple compelling reasons why XXXX is part and parcel of an individual’s life.)

On the one hand ,

On the other hand ,

Compared to their (adj ) counterparts, such individuals often v, thereby leading to a XXXX(richer) and XXXX(more productive) life

Accordingly , both factors at play stand as testament to the merits(value) of XXXX

总结

In conclusion, as an indispensable component of people’s lives. XXXX can deliver enormous and tangible benefits . In this case , only when we exert our utmost effort to XXXX can we carve out a more enriched and fulfilling life.
